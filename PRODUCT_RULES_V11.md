# Churning Product Rules V1.1

## Core product
Churning is primarily a **cash optimization and reward-tracking product** for liquid cash outside long-term investments. Cards & Spending is optional and secondary.

## Opportunity categories
Core cash plan:
- checking / direct-deposit bonuses
- savings bonuses
- HYSA / savings yield
- debit-spend bonuses
- CDs / fixed-term cash opportunities

Optional Cards & Spending:
- debit rewards / spend bonuses
- credit-card welcome offers

Later phase:
- brokerage signup/deposit bonuses

## Recommendation rules
- Geography filtered before display.
- More accurate questionnaire answers improve results.
- Research Verified is not an absolute “safe” label.
- Missing important research fields blocks Research Verified.
- Top ranking uses estimated net value, taxes when known, opportunity cost, fees, liquidity, effort, eligibility/history and research confidence.
- Simple: generally 1–2 active moves.
- Balanced: generally 2–3 active moves and is the recommended default.
- Active: up to ~5 active opportunities, but only when combined cash/DD requirements fit.
- Multiple DD lanes are only surfaced aggressively when the user says payroll supports split deposits.
- Spending offers must fit normal spending; Churning does not recommend unnecessary spending solely for a bonus.

## Account lifecycle
- Never assume “bonus received = close account.”
- Show minimum/qualification/hold/payout rules before the user adds an offer.
- Tracker calculates the earliest review point using qualification, payout and minimum account age.
- If a recurring fee exists, show the fee and fee-waiver rule prominently.
- If fee can be waived and the account remains useful: Keep while useful.
- If fee cannot be justified after reward completion: Review for closure after safe-review date.
- No-fee account/HYSA: Keep optional unless current terms or strategy provide a reason to change.
- Credit card with annual fee: review keep/downgrade/close before renewal; never automatic closure.
- Reward is only counted as earned after the user confirms actual payout.

## Optional card helper
- Not placed in the main recommendation flow.
- User must intentionally open Cards & Spending.
- Credit score asked only there, with “I don’t know.”
- Supports “I do not currently have a credit card.”
- Lets user search/select cards already held or recently held.
- Credit-card matches require a pay-in-full acknowledgement.
- Points show conservative cash value separately from possible travel value.
- Approval is never guaranteed.

## Research rules
- Official sources first.
- Secondary/community evidence may supplement facts a bank does not disclose, but must be labeled as secondary/not institution-confirmed.
- Deposit products require FDIC/NCUA or clearly researched program-bank insurance structure to become Research Verified.
- New offers normally enter Hold/Needs Review before publication.
- Changed terms automatically return to Hold.
- No CAPTCHA/bot-block bypassing.
- Weekly full research plus daily checks for stored offers expiring within 14 days.
- Later V2 adds discovery of brand-new offers across the web.

## Disclosure
Churning provides educational comparison, research and tracking tools. It is not a bank, broker, lender, investment adviser, tax adviser, legal adviser, or credit-repair service. Official institution terms control eligibility, approval, fees, rates and rewards.
